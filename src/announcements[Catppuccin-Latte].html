About- NARAYANA PUBLIC SCHOOL GONDA

Narayana Public school,Gonda is one of the most reputed school in Gonda district, Uttar Pradesh. Established with a vision to provide modern education from playgroup to Class 12th on CBSE pattern.At NPS,we believe education is not just about marks but about character building. The school has quickly emerged as the first choice of parents for its disciplined environment and excellent academic results.Our vision is to create responsible and confident citizens who are rooted in Indian culture but also prepared for global challenges.Our highly qualified teachers, well equipped laboratories,rich libarary and large sport ground ,safe transport covering all areas of Gonda provide the best environment for overall growth.What makes NPS truly different is to focus on co- curricular activities - debate,yoga,quiz,dance, music and its unique students journalism platform "NARAYANA NEWS". Here students learn to speak, to lead and to express.With 100% CBSE board result and top position in inter school competition,NPS is not just an school but a family where every child talent is recognised and nurtured.
