About Us - Narayana Public School Gonda

Narayana Public School, Gonda is one of the most reputed schools in Gonda district, Uttar Pradesh. Established with a vision to provide modern education from playgroup to Class 12th on the CBSE pattern. At NPS, we believe education is not just about marks but about character building.

The school has quickly emerged as the first choice of parents for its disciplined environment and excellent academic results. Our vision is to create responsible and confident citizens who are rooted in Indian culture but prepared for global challenges.

Our highly qualified teachers, well-equipped laboratories, rich library, large sports ground, and safe transport covering all areas of Gonda provide the best environment for overall growth.

What makes NPS truly different is its focus on co-curricular activities—debate, quiz, dance, music, yoga—and its unique student journalism platform, "Narayana News". Here, students learn to speak, to lead, and to express.

With a strong CBSE board record and top positions in interschool competitions, NPS is not just a school but a family where every child's talent is recognised and nurtured.
