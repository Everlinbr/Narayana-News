12. Inter-School Language Quiz Competition - 20 August 2026

An Inter-School Language-based Quiz Competition was held on 20 August 2026 in the auditorium of Narayana Public School. The event brought together students from four schools — Narayana Public School, Fatima, St. Xavier’s, and AIMS — in an engaging competition centred on language and literature.

The competition was conducted in two sections, Hindi and English, with the Hindi section taking place first, followed by the English section. The participants competed through a variety of interesting rounds, including Rapid Fire, Guess the Poet, and the Grammar Quiz. Questions were presented in different formats, requiring the teams to think quickly, recall information accurately, and work effectively together. The atmosphere remained lively and competitive throughout the event.

The quiz provided students with an opportunity to demonstrate their knowledge of language, grammar, and literature, while also developing quick thinking, teamwork, and confidence.

At the conclusion of the competition, Narayana Public School emerged as the winner. The achievement was celebrated with the presentation of a trophy and gifts to the winning team.

The smooth and successful conduct of the event was made possible through the guidance of the Principal, the support of the Vice-Principal, and the dedicated efforts of the teachers.
